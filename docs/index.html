Intake Composition Atlas

Populations exposed to inferences and decisions composed outside their effective contestation surface. Narrow federal intake surfaces — a portal, a single form — compose into a broader identity and decision graph. This is a typed, receipt-backed map of that composition: every edge carries exactly one claim type and a citation to a public record. Sibling to the Grid Dependency Atlas.
