# Provenance

This project is human-directed and AI-assisted. Final design authority, acceptance
criteria, and editorial control rest with the human author. AI contributions were
material and are categorized below by function.

## Human authorship

The author defined the project thesis, scope, schema design decisions, claim-type
doctrine, and editorial framing. AI systems contributed research, case drafting,
synthesis, and implementation under author supervision; they did not independently
determine project goals or publication decisions. The author reviewed, revised, or
rejected AI-generated output throughout development.

## AI-assisted collaboration

### Thesis framing and editorial critique

The thesis (composition outside the contestation surface), the insistence on typed
edges, the `signed_is_not_witnessed` rule, the administration-agnostic frame, and
the documented-only default view were developed in collaboration across model
sessions, including critique passes.

### Schema, linter, case research, and implementation

Lead collaboration: Claude (Anthropic) via Claude Code. Contributions to schema
design, the doctrine linter (`tools/lint.py`), receipt gathering from public
filings, case YAML assembly, the cytoscape.js graph view, and documentation.

## Provenance basis and limits

This document is a functional attribution record based on commit history,
co-author trailers, and the working conversation. It is not a complete forensic
account of all contributions. Some AI contributions (design critique, rejected
alternatives, sequencing) leave no repository artifact and cannot be fully
accounted for here. Model names/tools are recorded at the platform level; exact
model versions may vary across sessions.

## What this document does not claim

- No exact proportional attribution. Contributions are categorized by function,
  not quantified by token count or lines produced.
- "Ideas that didn't ship" and "approaches rejected" are real contributions that
  leave no artifact. This document cannot fully account for them.
